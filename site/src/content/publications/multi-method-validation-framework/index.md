abstract: >-
  To distinguish genuine findings from methodological artifacts, this paper proposes a validation
  framework based on method-invariant patterns. Analyzing 999,152 multilingual reviews across 18
  independent techniques (from classical clustering to Transformers), we demonstrate that
  substantive content accounts for 95.4% of variance, while methodological choice explains less than
  3%. The study confirms that robust patterns transcend specific algorithms and implementations.
  Furthermore, while BERT achieves peak accuracy (91.3%), classical approaches like SVM offer
  comparable performance (89.1%) with a 29-fold reduction in computational cost.
