abstract: >-
  This thesis addresses the challenge of portfolio optimization in high-dimensional settings (p > n)
  affected by sparse contamination (outliers). By proposing a robust Factor-Analytic approach, the
  study demonstrates how to stabilize covariance matrix estimation against data anomalies. The
  resulting allocation strategies exhibit superior resilience and risk-adjusted returns compared to
  traditional Mean-Variance frameworks, especially during market shocks.
